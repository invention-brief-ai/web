# Invention Brief Worksheet

From Invention Brief AI — a consultation-preparation tool.

This worksheet is general information to help you organize your idea before
speaking with a qualified patent professional. It is not legal advice, not a
patentability assessment, and not a filing document. Keep it for yourself and
the qualified professional you choose to work with — do not share unpublished
invention details with anyone else.

---

## 1. Working title

A short name for the invention, just for reference.

>

## 2. One-paragraph summary

Describe the invention in plain language, as if to a friend outside your field.

>

## 3. Problem

- Who has the problem, and in what situation?
- How do people deal with it today?
- Why are the current approaches not good enough?

>

## 4. Solution

- What are the main components or steps?
- How do they work together?
- Which parts are essential? Which are optional variations?
- Are there alternative ways to achieve the same result?

>

## 5. Effects / advantages

What measurably improves, and for whom?

>

## 6. Use cases

Where, when, and by whom would this be used?

>

## 7. Known similar approaches

- The closest existing products, services, or methods you know about
- How yours differs from each (honestly — "closest is X, mine differs in Y")
- Your own related earlier work

>

## 8. Disclosure record

Every public disclosure so far — demos, talks, articles, social media, sales,
crowdfunding. For each: what, when, where, to whom. Also list planned
disclosures you could postpone.

>

## 9. People and context

- Everyone who contributed to the invention
- Employment or collaboration context that might be relevant

>

## 10. Goals and constraints

Target markets/countries, rough timeline, budget range.

>

## 11. Questions for my patent professional

What you want to ask in the first consultation — options, what to avoid,
what to document, fees, confidentiality, next steps.

>

---

Invention Brief AI is a consultation-preparation tool. It does not provide
legal advice, patentability opinions, claim drafting, patent application
drafting, or patent filing services. Always consult a qualified patent
professional for legal advice and filing decisions.
